<analysis>**original_problem_statement:**
Build me a billing system that has the following features.

A. **Backend for Admin:**
1.  **Login:** Admin login page.
2.  **Dashboard:** Gross sales, expenses, net sales, monthly sales graph, receivables, active subscribers, open tickets, ticket of the month, Mikrotik stats (CPU, RAM usage, etc.), recent activity.
3.  **User Management:** Create/manage user accounts with roles (cashier, tech, admin, user, billing).
4.  **Settings:** Discounts/rebates, Subscription Plans, import/export.
5.  **Mikrotik Management (v6 & v7):** Integration/configuration.
6.  **Creation of Mikrotik Accounts:** Integrated with subscriber registration (PPPoE username, password, profile) with an option to activate on save. Sync accounts from Mikrotik, update profiles.
7.  **Subscriber Registration:**
    *   **Data:** Auto-generated account number, PPPoE account, billing period, installation details, plan, one-time charges.
    *   **Actions:** Job order creation, pro-rated bill, modem assignment, next billing schedule.
    *   **Management:** Edit, Activate/Deactivate, View payment/billing history, job orders, logs, equipment used.
    *   **Address:** Split into dropdowns for Province, Municipality, Barangay, and a text field for Street.
8.  **Company Setup:** Logo, business name, address, email, mobile for receipts/SOA.
9.  **Inventory:** Stock management, MAC/serial tracking, bulk item tracking (e.g., fiber cable usage), costing.
10. **Expenses:** Category-based, recurring, included in dashboard net sales.
11. **Import/Export:** Subscribers.
12. **Billing Module:**
    *   **SOA:** PDF generator, printable, auto-email.
    *   **Billing Cycle:** Automatic invoice generation.
    *   **Automatic SMS:** Triggers for bill generation, payment received, overdue, disconnection.

B. **Cashier Module:**
*   Login, dashboard, fast search, receipt printing, OR/SI numbering, audit trail, mode of payment, advance payment logic (wallet/credit ledger).

C. **Technician Module:**
*   Login, dashboard (job order stats), fulfill job orders with material entry.

D. **Subscribers Portal:**
*   Login (account number), dashboard (job orders, payment records, chat, account history).

**User's preferred language**: English

    *   **Cashier:** The dashboard features a fully functional centralized payment system.
